Research profile

Computational mechanics researcher working on the geometry side of structural analysis and design: topology and shape optimization, shell and solid element formulations, adjoint sensitivities, and the verification that establishes whether a computed result can be trusted. Doctoral work produced a unified geometry projection method for additively manufactured variable stiffness composite laminates, published as first author in Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering. Around 126 citations, h-index 5, i10-index 4 on Google Scholar.
