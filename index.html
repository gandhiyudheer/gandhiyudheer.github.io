About

Started in mechanical engineering in Bengaluru. Got obsessed with how composites fail in Parma. Spent four years in Forlì inventing better ways to design them. Now in Tokyo, building the tools other engineers will use.

My PhD at the University of Bologna focused on geometry-projection methods for the topology optimization of additively manufactured, variable-stiffness composite laminates — a mouthful of a thesis title that boils down to: given a 3D printer that lays down continuous fiber, what's the best way to route the fibers? The methods I developed are published in Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering, and they're being used on real parts — including components for the Ferrari 296GTB and the Emilia V solar vehicle that won the Sasol 2024 Cruiser Challenge.

At Braid Technologies in Tokyo, I work on the mathematical models and simulation infrastructure that power automated design workflows for industrial R&D clients. Day to day, that means finite element analysis across the full spectrum — linear, nonlinear, implicit, explicit — topology optimization, and integrating open-source solvers into a unified engine that design engineers actually want to use.

What keeps me interested: the elegance of math that reshapes matter, the satisfaction of watching research show up in real cars, building tools that outlast the projects they were built for, and the messier truth that most weeks are about solving whatever's broken.

    Translational R&D on topology optimization for continuous fiber-reinforced polymer components, bridging academic methods with industrial 3D-printing workflows.

    • Developed TO methods for CFRP targeting Markforged and 9tlabs printers within the ASCENT project.
    • Co-led design, analysis, and 3D printing of parts for the Emilia V solar vehicle — winner of the Sasol 2024 Cruiser Challenge.
    • Built a stress-based design framework for 3D-printed Ferrari 296GTB wing mechanism parts within the OptiFiber project, in collaboration with Ferrari.
    CFRP design Continuous-fiber 3D printing Markforged / 9tlabs Ferrari · Emilia collaborations
  3. Apr 2018 — Feb 2020 Parma, Italy

    Research Associate · University of Parma

    First real immersion in computational composites. Modeled the thermo-mechanical response of shape memory alloys via UMAT subroutines in Abaqus, and developed a fiber optimization method to predict bistable laminate configurations. In parallel, worked on the initiation and evolution of delamination in composites using VCCT and cohesive-element techniques.

    Shape memory alloys Bistable laminates Delamination · VCCT Abaqus UMAT Fortran
  4. Apr — Sep 2017 Coventry, UK

    Visiting Graduate Researcher · Coventry University

    A detour from solids into robotics that turned out to shape how I think about optimization. Developed A* and D* path-planning and obstacle-avoidance algorithms for a rover operating under kinematic and dynamic constraints, and implemented an online nonlinear controller based on Particle Swarm Optimization for trajectory traversal.

    Path planning Nonlinear control Particle Swarm Optimization
